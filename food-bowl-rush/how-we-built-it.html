Our Game Idea
Where Food Bowl Rush was born!

It all started with a question: "What if food could race?"

We imagined a world where your favourite foods — crabs, ramen noodles, pizza slices, and even boba tea — could hop along a track trying to land perfectly in a big bowl at the end. Jump too short and you'll never get there. But jump too far... and you OVERSHOOT! 😬

The Big Idea 🎯

🏁 The Race: Two players each pick a food character. They press their key to make the food jump. First one to land IN the bowl zone wins — but not too far!
🥣 The Bowl Zone: There's a golden zone near the end of the track. Land inside it and you win! But go even one pixel further and your food flies off into space. Poof! 💨
✨ Special Powers: Each food has its own personality. The Crab sometimes shuffles backwards! Sushi gets faster every jump. Donut saves up for a massive 3rd jump. Every food is different!

What Makes It Fun? 🎉

  • Randomness — you never quite know how far you'll jump!
  • Personality — each food fighter has a unique trick
  • Tension — when you're close to the bowl, every press matters
  • Laughter — watching the Crab shuffle backwards is hilarious

Step 3: The Jump Mechanic
The heart of the game — how each press moves your food!

Every time you press your key, the game calls a function called calcJump() which figures out how far your food will hop this time. Then applyJump() moves it and checks whether you won, overshot, or just moved forward!

The Track — How Positions Work 📏

The track is a number line from 0 (start) to 100 (end). Your food lives at a position number. When you jump, we add the jump distance to your position.

🏁 THE RACE TRACK
🚀 🦀 🍜 🏆 🥣
pos = 0 (start) pos 92–100 = WIN ZONE! 🥣 pos >100 = OVERSHOOT 💨

Three Things That Can Happen 🎲

✅ Normal Jump
pos + jump < 92: Move forward, update progress bar, play boing sound!
🏆 Win!
92 ≤ pos + jump ≤ 100: Land in bowl zone! Play fanfare, show winner screen!
💨 Overshoot!
pos + jump > 100: Too far! Food flies off screen. Elimination!

Each Food's Jump Style 🍱

"Each food has its own special jump style!"
We wrote a big switch statement — it's like a set of rules, one for each food. Depending on which food you picked, the jump calculation is completely different. That's what makes the game feel alive and fun!

Step 4: Special Powers in Code
How we turned game ideas into real JavaScript!

Each food's special power is just a few lines of code inside the calcJump() function. Here's how we turned each idea into reality:

Food Special Power How It Works in Code
🦀 Crabwalk Use Math.random() < 0.30 — if true, return a negative number to go backwards!
🍜 Slurp Boost 25% chance to multiply the jump by × 1.75 — that's 75% extra distance!
🍠 Crispy Hop Narrow random range: rnd(9, 15) — always between 9 and 15
🌮 Shell Spin Wide random range: rnd(2, 26) — could be tiny or huge!
🍕 Cheese Drag 25% chance to return 0 — zero movement, completely stuck!
🍣 Rolling Rush Track how many jumps: bonus = jumps × 1.6. More jumps = faster! (capped at +13)
🍩 Sugar Rush Check jumps % 3 === 2 — every third jump triggers the big one (19–24)!
🌭 Stick Launch 15% chance (Math.random() < 0.15) for a mega jump of 23–30
🥟 Steam Pop Return an array [jump1, jump2] — the game detects this and fires two jumps!
🍦 Brain Freeze Set a _frozen = true flag — the game checks this first and plays freeze animation
🧋 Bubble Pop Widest range of all: rnd(2, 27) — pure chaos! 🫧
🍩 Fun fact about Sushi: "Sushi gets faster each jump because we add a bonus each time! The formula is bonus = jumps × 1.6, but we cap it at 13 so it doesn't get TOO crazy. Early in the race Sushi is slow — but near the end, watch out!"

Quick Rules 📜

  • Pick your food fighter — each one has a unique jumping style
  • Press your key to make your food hop down the track
  • Land in the golden bowl zone (between position 92 and 100) to WIN
  • Don't overshoot! Going past position 100 means you're eliminated
  • There's a cooldown between jumps — no button mashing! (380ms)
  • In 4 or 6 player mode, it's team vs team — whole team must be out to lose

The Winner Screen 🏆

When someone wins, the game shows the winner screen with:

  • The winning food emoji bouncing up and down 🎉
  • Confetti raining down in all colours
  • A fun fanfare sound (ascending musical notes!)
  • The food's name and which player gets the bowl

What We Learned!
"We built a whole game in one afternoon!"

Building Food Bowl Rush taught us so many things about how computers and games work. Here are the big ideas we picked up:

🏗️
HTML
HTML is the skeleton of a webpage. We used it to create all the buttons, screens, tracks, and food emojis.
🎨
CSS
CSS makes things look beautiful. We used gradients, animations, and transitions to bring the game to life!
⚡
JavaScript
JS makes things happen. Key presses, jumps, random numbers, timers — all JavaScript!
🎲
Randomness
Math.random() gives a number between 0 and 1. We use it to make every jump feel different and exciting!
🔊
Web Audio
We made all the sounds using the Web Audio API — no sound files needed! We built boing sounds from scratch using oscillators.
🎮
Game Design
We learned that good games need clear rules, fair chances, and moments of surprise. Food Bowl Rush has all three!
🚀 The coolest thing we built: The sound effects are made entirely in code using oscillators — these are mathematical waves that make different pitches. The "boing" sound starts at a high pitch (280 Hz) and drops to a low pitch (140 Hz) really fast. That falling pitch is what makes it sound bouncy!
